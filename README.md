## Featured Projects
 
### [mlb-pitch-framing-analysis](https://github.com/jameschen108/mlb-pitch-framing-analysis)
 
Measures how much of a catcher's extra strike calls belongs to him rather than to the
umpire and pitcher he happens to work with. A GAM over pitch location supplies the
counterfactual, and a crossed random-effects model splits the residual three ways.
1.06M called pitches, 2021–2023.
 
`Python` · `logistic GAM` · `crossed random effects` · `Statcast`
 
---
 
### [tw-stock-lstm-evaluation](https://github.com/jameschen108/tw-stock-lstm-evaluation)
 
Trains an LSTM to predict next-day Taiwan stock prices, then asks whether the predictions
are worth anything: walk-forward evaluation against a "tomorrow = today" baseline, a
feature ablation, and a long/flat backtest with Taiwan retail trading costs.
 
`TensorFlow/Keras` · `walk-forward evaluation` · `FastAPI` · `Docker`

### [food-calorie-vlm](https://github.com/jameschen108/food-calorie-vlm)

### [santander-transaction-prediction](https://github.com/jameschen108/santander-transaction-prediction)
 
Compares logistic regression against an MLP on identical folds, with per-fold variation
reported so the gap can be read against the noise. Separately reproduces the competition's

`LightGBM` · `scikit-learn` · `stratified k-fold`
